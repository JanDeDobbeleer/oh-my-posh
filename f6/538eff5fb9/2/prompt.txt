**Key corrections to the prior run's picture:**
- The prior "nohttp = 21,030,888" claim was wrong: full net/http+TLS elimination measures 26.09 MB. The 21 MB figure matches nohttp+registry combined. HTTP is worth 3.26 MB raw, not 8 MB.
- Savings from nohttp and the empty registry are additive within 3.4 KB.
- The dsc→cobra→jsonschema→yaml/v4 chain is only 0.26 MB raw / 37 KB brotli.
- `go tool nm` cannot parse wasm at all (go1.26) — attribution was done via a native proxy binary built from an overlay-added `src/attrib/main.go` mirroring wasm/main.go. TLS removal verified there: 2,469 bytes of tls stringers remain, zero transport/handshake symbols.
- The registry's gob init (117 `gob.Register` calls) pins segments just as hard as the map — emptying the map alone is insufficient.
- Bonus: go-runewidth's `strictWidthLUT` is 2.2 MB of BSS filled by ~2.23M function calls in package init at every wasm instantiation — a startup-latency issue, not a size issue.
